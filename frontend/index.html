ABOUT VERISHIELD

Making package compliance easier to check.

Checking packaged commodities manually can require reviewing multiple declarations, label details and applicable requirements.

VeriShield is designed as a digital screening system that helps organize this process by scanning package images, extracting visible information and checking it against structured compliance rules.

Frequently asked questions.

A few important things to know about VeriShield.

VeriShield is a software system for preliminary screening of packaged commodities against supported Legal Metrology requirements using package images, OCR, structured information and rule-based checking.
The system processes the package image, extracts visible text, maps relevant information into structured fields and evaluates applicable rules to identify detected declarations and potential findings.
No. VeriShield is intended to assist preliminary screening and organize information. Final legal interpretation and inspection decisions remain with the appropriate authority or qualified professional.
No. VeriShield does not provide legal certification. Results should be treated as preliminary compliance screening, and ambiguous findings may require manual verification.
